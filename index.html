            How StampDesk works

            1. Everything runs in this browser. No account, no server, no API key, no subscription. Your stamps and documents never leave this computer.
            2. Add a company, then upload a photo or scan of your stamp or signature. The white paper is removed automatically, leaving only the ink.
            3. Open a PDF and drag the stamp exactly where it belongs. Resize from the corners or the side handles, rotate, set opacity, align to the page, or type exact sizes in mm, cm, inches or points.
            4. Bulk Stamp stamps many PDFs at once: place the stamp once, and smart placement finds the same spot (e.g. next to “Authorised Signatory”) on every page and file.
            5. Edit a stamp any time from the library — rename it, move it to another company, replace the image, rotate, trim or darken the ink.
            6. Export — the stamped PDF is built here and downloads straight to your computer.
            7. Take a backup from the History tab so you can move your library to another computer.

            This places an image on a page. It is not a cryptographic digital signature (DSC / PAdES) and does not by itself establish legal validity.
